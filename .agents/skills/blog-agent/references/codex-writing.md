# Conversational article writing

Use this for a new article or substantive body rewrite. A bounded correction to a user-approved manuscript can be
edited directly. Preserve a user-designated final manuscript unless asked to
change it. Image-generation rules remain project-owned.

1. Confirm the article's query, reader, purpose and scope. Finish the applicable
   SERP/structure gates before writing; reuse valid GSC evidence under
   [gsc-evidence.md](gsc-evidence.md). For each planned H2/H3, inspect the
   search results for that section's distinct reader intent or query, reusing
   current evidence where it answers the same question. Record the query,
   source, retrieval time, observed result types, relevant pages and the reason
   to include, merge, rename or omit the heading. Keep ads and SERP features
   separate from organic results. Avoid copying a heading just because one
   competitor uses it, and avoid a separate search for synonymous headings
   with the same intent. Apply [title-structure-rules.md](title-structure-rules.md).
2. Draft in the current Codex session from the approved research and reader
   questions. Ask what the reader would need to know next; answer in ordinary
   language before assembling the article. If the project explicitly authorizes
   a separate writing model, use its existing safe route and cost limits. Keep
   its answers as draft material, never as factual sources or an approved manuscript.
3. Codex checks externally verifiable claims, especially figures, dates,
   eligibility, rules, prices and sweeping statements, against applicable
   primary sources. Remove unsupported claims or correct them with a recorded
   source. Never invent an experience, quote or measured result. Assemble the
   verified material as an article, preserving the conversation's ordinary
   wording, short paragraphs and useful order where possible. Do not present
   the exchange as Q&A or add SEO-style filler. Use
   [Japanese writing review](japanese-writing.md) for the
   final Japanese edit.
4. For a new article or full rewrite, write an introduction of roughly 300
   Japanese characters before the first H2. Answer the main concern promptly,
   show who the article helps and what follows, and keep its claims source-safe.
   Do not pad to an exact count. A small passage edit need not create a new
   introduction. Headings serve the SERP-tested structure; paragraph language
   stays conversational. Apply [article-production.md](article-production.md)
   and project-specific length requirements to the complete draft.
5. Check the finished article's intent, factual claims, introduction, heading
   evidence, links, length and rendering. Save through the existing project
   adapter and verify readback. Keep draft completion, user adoption,
   publication and delivery separate; existing authorization controls later
   steps.

If an optional writing-model call fails, preserve completed research and
transcripts, record the failure and cost state, then continue drafting in Codex
when the project permits. Do not overwrite an unknown write or clear an
unresolved reservation.
