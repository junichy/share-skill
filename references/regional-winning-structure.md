# Regional Winning Structure

Use this reference when planning, writing, or rewriting area-based SEO articles such as:

- `<service> 地域`
- `<service> 地域 おすすめ`
- `<service> 地域 料金`
- `<service> 地域 比較`
- `<service> 地域 メイク`
- `<service> 地域 写真館`

The goal is not to make a generic local guide. The goal is to create a page that helps the reader choose a concrete provider, store, studio, facility, or next action in that area.

## Core Principle

Do not lead with the editor's internal area classification.

Weak framing:

- 都心型 / 生活圏型
- 北部 / 南部 / 東部 / 西部
- エリア別に考える
- 地域ごとの特徴だけを説明する

These can be useful as supporting filters, but they are rarely the main reason a regional article wins.

Strong framing:

- Compare real providers/stores/facilities in the region.
- Show real prices or price ranges.
- Explain what is included and what costs extra.
- Give each listing enough information to be useful by itself.
- Route the reader to the right official/store/detail page.

## Winning Pattern

High-performing regional roundup/media articles across genres tend to share these parts:

1. Early answer: what the reader can decide from this page.
2. Fast comparison table: names, area/access, price, included service, time, official link.
3. Thick provider cards: image, description, pricing, access, hours, inclusions, next action.
4. Price explanation: not only cheap/expensive, but what creates the total cost.
5. Selection criteria: how to choose without regret.
6. Condition/area routes: station, city, nearby area, service condition, reader type.
7. Optional question block: use only when a distinct search-intent variant remains unanswered in the main flow.
8. Final next action: store list, official page, inquiry, related article, or parent guide.

## Required SERP Work

For a regional keyword, do not assume the article type from the keyword alone.

Before title or outline:

1. Retrieve the exact query with the approved keyword source following [keyword-source-research.md](keyword-source-research.md). Use [serp-browser-evidence.md](serp-browser-evidence.md) only when local/map layout, location-specific screen results or another actual-screen claim needs observation.
2. Save the organic top 10 with URL, title, page type, and SERP features.
3. Mark whether winners are:
   - local provider LPs
   - large roundup media
   - marketplace/category pages
   - official data pages
   - Q&A/news pages
4. For top 5, inspect structure deeply:
   - H1
   - main H2/H3
   - comparison table presence
   - provider cards
   - prices
   - images
   - CTA
   - trust signals
   - Optional question block when needed
   - lower-area links
5. Decide whether the article should be:
   - provider roundup/comparison
   - local service guide
   - marketplace/category support page
   - single provider LP support
   - parent hub with child regional pages

If the top SERP is mostly provider LPs, an article can still compete only if it has a clear roundup advantage: multiple providers, prices, comparison axes, and useful decision support.

If essential keyword-source/competitor evidence is missing, record the blocker and keep the dependent title or outline decision unresolved. If only Google screen evidence is unavailable, withhold screen-specific claims; source-labelled research can continue. Do not equate national or unknown-location rank data with a particular local screen.

If an individual top result cannot be inspected after the SERP itself was captured, record it as `not inspectable` with the reason. You may try one lightweight fallback, such as HTML read or another browser surface, to identify page type and obvious structure. Do not count that page as fully inspected unless the main H1/H2, comparison/table presence, price/provider evidence, CTA, and trust signals are actually visible.

## Mixed SERP Scope Decision

Many regional SERPs mix different provider types. Decide the article scope before writing the outline.

Examples:

- clinic vs self-service store
- provider LP vs roundup media vs marketplace
- official price database vs editorial recommendation page
- in-studio service vs 출장/online service
- senior-specialist provider vs general provider

Create a short scope table:

| SERP type | Include deeply | Mention only | Exclude | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Then state the final scope in one sentence, such as:

- `This article compares photo studios with in-studio senior/memorial portrait plans; 출장撮影 is mentioned only as an option.`
- `This article compares dental-clinic whitening; self-whitening salons are mentioned separately because the provider type and legal/service expectations differ.`

Do not mix provider types only to increase the number of listings. A thinner but coherent comparison is better than a broad list with incompatible prices and service definitions.

## Title Rules

Avoid lazy symbol-separated titles such as `A｜B｜C` unless the site has a strong local convention for them.

Prefer natural titles:

- `大阪で生前遺影撮影できる写真館の料金と選び方`
- `埼玉で遺影撮影できる写真館の料金と選び方`
- `千葉でメイク付き遺影撮影を相談できる写真館の選び方`

Title should contain:

- region
- service/action
- provider type when useful
- decision axis such as price, selection, comparison, or included service

## Intro Requirements

The intro must quickly answer:

1. What can be found in this area.
2. What the page compares.
3. What data was checked and when if the article uses provider/store information.
4. Which reader action comes next.

Avoid starting with abstract regional commentary. Do not spend the first section explaining the author's area taxonomy.

## Comparison Table

A regional article should usually include an early table.

Recommended columns:

- Provider/store/facility name
- Area or nearest station
- Price or price range
- Included services
- Time or appointment length when relevant
- Photos/examples available
- Family/group support when relevant
- Official/detail page link

For photo/studio articles, useful columns include:

- 店舗名
- エリア/最寄り
- 料金目安
- ヘアメイク
- 家族写真
- 所要時間
- データ/プリント
- 店舗ページ

## Provider Card Requirements

Each provider/store card should be thick enough to make a decision.

Include when available:

- image from an authorized source
- provider/store name
- one-sentence fit statement
- address
- nearest station/access
- hours/closed days
- price range
- plan names
- what each plan includes
- options or extra costs
- examples/gallery if available
- trust signal such as specialist focus, review count, official listing, or editorial check date
- CTA/detail link

Do not scrape or reuse third-party images unless usage rights are clear. Prefer owned media, official media, embedded store pages, or manually approved assets.

## Price Section

Regional articles often win by explaining total cost, not just listing a low starting price.

Break down:

- base fee
- included service
- optional services
- data/delivery
- prints/materials
- consultation/reservation costs
- cancellation or weekend fees where relevant
- time burden

For memorial/studio articles, break down:

- 撮影料
- ヘアメイク
- レタッチ/修整
- データ
- プリント
- 台紙/額
- 家族写真
- 土日料金/追加料金
- 所要時間

## Internal Links

Regional articles should not be isolated.

Plan links by role:

- area listing page
- individual provider/store pages
- nearby region articles
- parent service guide
- price article
- preparation article
- condition-specific article
- final store search or inquiry CTA

Links must appear where the reader naturally needs them, not only in a bottom related block.

## Optional Question Prompts

Regional question blocks are optional. Use them only to answer a distinct query variant or decision blocker that does not fit naturally in the main sections.

Good question-block types:

- cheapest/price range
- what is included
- how to choose
- whether a service is available in the area
- whether family/group support is possible
- whether makeup/options are included
- how long it takes
- what to bring
- whether nearby regions should also be checked

## Red Flags

Fail or rewrite the outline if:

- The article starts with only area theory.
- The first useful provider/store info appears too late.
- It has no comparison table.
- It has provider names but no prices or concrete decision axes.
- It has prices but does not explain what is included.
- Every provider card is a thin one-line summary.
- It uses "おすすめ" without explaining why each listing is recommended.
- It has no official/detail links.
- It lacks trust signals or checked-date context for provider data.
- It copies a competitor's order, copy, or proprietary scoring.

## Final Structure Template

Use this as the default shape, then adapt to the actual SERP.

1. Intro: what this region article helps decide.
2. Key points: 2-4 non-TOC value statements.
3. Early comparison table.
4. H2: choosing by price and included service.
5. H2: providers/stores/facilities in the region.
   - H3 provider card 1
   - H3 provider card 2
   - H3 provider card 3
6. H2: price/total cost explanation.
7. H2: selection criteria and common mistakes.
8. H2: options, preparation, or condition-specific decisions.
9. H2: reservation/checklist.
10. H2: Optional question block only when a distinct blocker remains.
11. Summary with next action and internal links.
